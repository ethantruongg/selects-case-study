Product decisions

Deciding what to include and prioritize in V1 required a very intentional scope.

I kept the first version focused on the moments that mattered most in the photographer-client workflow, then made deliberate tradeoffs around usability, security, and operational control.

01 · SELECTING

Three ways to review

Grid, swipe, and lightbox modes share one live selection state, so clients can switch interaction models without losing their place.

02 · PRICING

Dynamic pricing

Fully customizable pricing models with dynamic pricing options allows for an easier and more transparent client experience.

03 · SECURITY

Access without accounts

Private links and PIN verification reduce login friction while rate limiting and temporary lockouts protect against brute-force access.

04 · MEDIA

Auto-watermark proofs

Automatically generate watermarked previews at upload, allowing for a faster and cleaner post-shoot client workflow.

05 · DELIVERY

Release when you want

A manual toggle separates photographer workflow from client visibility, giving the photographer more control over the photos shipped.

06 · DEVICES

Download your photos

Desktop clients get ZIP delivery while mobile clients use the native share sheet, allowing clients to use the platform effectively regardless of device.

Product walkthrough

Two sides of the same workflow.

Selects connects the client selection experience with the photographer workflow, from initial gallery access through final delivery.

01 · Client experience

From opening the gallery to receiving the final photos.

The client flow is designed to reduce friction at each step while making selection, pricing, and delivery clear.

01 · Client

Access your secure gallery.

Clients can enter through a private link and a PIN. Lost your link? Look up a gallery using a name and PIN. Selects preserves privacy without requiring an account.

02 · Client

Review photos your way.

Grid, swipe, and full-screen lightbox views support different browsing behaviors while maintaining one shared selection state across the gallery.

03 · Client

Know what you're spending.

Pricing updates automatically as selections change, so clients can understand the cost of adding photos before they finalize and submit their choices.

04 · Client

Review and download your final selection.

After the photographer releases the finished gallery, clients can review their selection of full-resolution photos and download the delivered files directly onto their device.

02 · Photographer experience

Behind every client gallery is a controlled workflow.

The photographer side keeps shoots organized and low-friction across upload, pricing, delivery, and shoot-management.

01 · Photographer

Keep your workspace private.

Photographer tools sit behind authenticated access, separating the operational dashboard from the lightweight client experience.

02 · Photographer

Set pricing for each shoot.

Each gallery can reflect its own included-photo count and dynamic pricing structure, supporting different shoots without hard-coding one commercial model.

03 · Photographer

Upload and protect your proofs.

Upload creates protected watermarked client previews while keeping original files private, building proof protection into the workflow before a gallery is shared.

04 · Photographer

Control when photos go live.

A manual release step allows the photographer time to review the work before full-resolution files become available to the client.

05 · Photographer

Manage every shoot in one place.

The dashboard provides a single view of active shoots and their status, turning the gallery from a one-off client page into a repeatable operating workflow.

Research → prioritization → iteration

Built with real workflows in mind.

I collected feedback from clients and photographers across both sides of the workflow, then used that feedback to prioritize improvements to photo selection and gallery management.

The goal was not to recreate every feature of a mature gallery platform. It was to understand where friction existed, make explicit product tradeoffs, and ship a focused v1.
